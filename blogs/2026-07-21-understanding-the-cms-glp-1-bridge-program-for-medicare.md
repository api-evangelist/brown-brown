The rising demand for GLP-1 medications — widely recognized for their effectiveness in weight management and chronic disease prevention — has created a significant coverage gap in Medicare. Historically, federal law has prohibited Medicare Part D from covering drugs prescribed solely for weight loss. The Medicare GLP-1 Bridge program represents Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)’s first major step toward addressing this gap.
