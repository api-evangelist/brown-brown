New Jersey recently enacted a new law ( Bill A5324 ) establishing the New Jersey Medicaid Employer Assessment which will require covered employers to pay a fee for each employee and dependent covered under New Jersey Medicaid. This law applies to employers with 50 or more employees enrolled in New Jersey Medicaid, including private employers and potentially public employers (regardless of employer location).
