Cargo theft is evolving. For years, transportation companies focused on identifying fraudulent carriers before they gained access to freight. They managed this by verifying the carrier's authority, confirming insurance, reviewing operating history, validating contact information, checking safety records, and using established carrier-vetting platforms.
