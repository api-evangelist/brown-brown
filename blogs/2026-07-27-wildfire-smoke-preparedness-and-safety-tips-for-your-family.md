Why wildfire smoke preparedness matters As wildfire activity continues across parts of North America this summer, many communities are experiencing smoky skies and poor air quality, even when active wildfires are hundreds of miles away. Smoke can travel long distances, affecting daily life, outdoor activities, and public health well beyond areas directly impacted by fire.
