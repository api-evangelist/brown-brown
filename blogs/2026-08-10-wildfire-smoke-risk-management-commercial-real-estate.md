Wildfire smoke does not stop at the fire line. In recent years, smoke plumes from large wildfires have blanketed cities hundreds of miles away, turning the air hazy and pushing particulate matter into buildings that have never faced a wildfire threat directly. Commercial real estate owners and property managers across the country, not just in the West, need to treat smoke as a routine operational and risk management concern.
