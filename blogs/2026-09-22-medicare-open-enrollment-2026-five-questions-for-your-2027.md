Medicare Open Enrollment is one of the most important times of the year for Medicare beneficiaries. From October 15 through December 7, you have the opportunity to review your current coverage and make changes that will take effect on January 1, 2027. During this period, you can switch Medicare Advantage plans, move between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage, enroll in a Part D prescription drug plan, or make changes to your existing drug coverage.
