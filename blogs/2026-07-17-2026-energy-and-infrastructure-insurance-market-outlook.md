Property insurance market trends Despite significant CAT activity in 2024, many insurance carriers maintained profitability, which contributed to a more stabilized market environment in 2025. As we entered 2026, the first quarter marked a notable and somewhat rapid shift in the property market — from prolonged hard market conditions to an increasingly softening environment. This transition has continued into Q2, with further softening across key lines.
