Healthcare costs continue to rise, and employers are under increasing pressure to manage expenses while still offering benefits that help employees and their families stay healthy, protected and financially secure. According to Brown & Brown’s 2026 Healthcare Cost Outlook , employers expect healthcare costs to increase an average of 10% in 2026 after plan offering changes, and 12% if no changes are made to plan offerings.
