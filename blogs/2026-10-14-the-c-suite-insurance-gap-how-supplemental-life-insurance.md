Why employer-paid life insurance can leave executives under protected Many senior leaders assume their employer-provided life insurance coverage is sufficient. After all, most organizations offer some form of group life insurance benefit as part of their overall compensation package. However, as careers advance and financial responsibilities become more complex, many executives discover that the protection they have in place may cover only a small portion of their actual exposure.
