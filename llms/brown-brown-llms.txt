# Brown & Brown

> Brown & Brown, Inc. (NYSE: BRO) is a Daytona Beach, Florida headquartered insurance brokerage and risk-management intermediary founded in 1939, and one of the largest independent brokers in the United States. It is a distributor, not a carrier: it places risk with insurers rather than underwriting it. Following the 2025 acquisition of Accession Risk Management Group (Risk Strategies and One80 Intermediaries) it reports through two segments — Retail (property and casualty, employee benefits, private client, captive solutions, financial and wealth services) and Specialty Distribution (programs, wholesale brokerage and MGA/binding-authority businesses including Arrowhead).

> API posture: Brown & Brown publishes NO public API, NO developer portal and NO machine-readable specification. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the catalog record, not published by Brown & Brown.

## API surface

- [No public API](https://us.bbrown.com/): There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC artifact, no public Postman workspace, no SDK and no webhook catalog on any Brown & Brown first-party property. developer.bbrown.com, developers.bbrown.com, docs.bbrown.com and api.bbrown.com do not resolve in DNS; /developers, /developer, /api, /partners and /integrations return 404 on both bbrown.com and us.bbrown.com.
- [Arrowhead Programs Enterprise API (EAPI)](https://arrowheadprograms.com/how-our-strategic-api-team-is-broadening-our-market-reach/): The only genuine programmatic surface in the group, and it is partner-gated. A quote-only rating integration over the Arrowhead Exchange agent platform (Specialty Distribution segment), delivered by commercial agreement to comparative raters and aggregators — CoverHound, Tarmika, Semsee, DAIS. No public documentation, no published base URL, no specification. api.arrowheadexchange.com resolves behind Cloudflare and returns 404 for the root and for every probed spec path.
- Insurance verbs: quote is exposed partner-only; bind, issue and FNOL are not exposed by any documented API.
- Carrier data exchange: no ACORD, AL3, ACORD XML or NGDS reference is published by Brown & Brown. Exchange runs through the agency management system tier — Brown & Brown is a publicly referenced Vertafore AMS360 agency — and IVANS agency download, which belongs to the AMS vendor and the carriers, not to a Brown & Brown interface.
